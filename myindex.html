Lost something on campus? Found something that isn’t yours?

CampusFind is a lost-and-found board that lives on the Ethereum Sepolia test network. Every report is stored by a smart contract, so records are public and cannot be quietly changed or deleted.

Report an item
  • Report a lost or found item in under a minute
  • Search every report by item name
  • Only the person who reported an item can mark it as claimed

How it works

  1. Connect your wallet. MetaMask identifies you. No account or password is needed.
  2. Report an item. Approve the transaction in MetaMask. It costs a tiny amount of free Sepolia test ETH.
  3. Browse and search. Everyone can see every report and filter by name.
  4. Mark it as claimed. When the item is back with its owner, the reporter closes the case.

Everything on a blockchain is public. Do not put phone numbers or other private details in a report.
